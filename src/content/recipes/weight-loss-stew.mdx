<div class="lang-en">

<ol class="steps">
<li><strong>Prep the aromatics</strong><p>Chop about four cloves of garlic and one onion into big pieces — this is a chunky stew, so keep everything with some bite.</p></li>
<li><strong>Start the base</strong><p>Heat a Dutch oven over medium with one tablespoon of olive oil — all the added fat for the whole pot. Add the onion and garlic and let them soften.</p></li>
<li><strong>Add lentils and tofu</strong><p>Rinse about ½ cup of lentils and stir them in. Drain the tofu (no need to press it) and break it into the pot in chunks.</p></li>
<li><strong>Season</strong><p>Add cumin, smoked paprika, salt, pepper, and about 2 tablespoons of low-sodium soy sauce. Let it cook a few minutes — the soy sauce builds a slow-cooked, umami flavor.</p></li>
<li><strong>Add the vegetables</strong><p>Chop the celery, potatoes, carrots, and remaining onion large, and clean the mushrooms. Add them all, then pour in the whole can of diced tomatoes. Let it cook down for a moment.</p></li>
<li><strong>Simmer</strong><p>Cover with just enough water to reach the top of the ingredients. Put the lid on, drop to low heat, and simmer 25–30 minutes until the lentils are soft and the broth has thickened.</p></li>
<li><strong>Finish with greens</strong><p>Stir in about two cups of spinach and let it wilt. Serve in big bowls — one serving is a huge, filling portion.</p></li>
</ol>

### Tips

- The formula matters more than the exact recipe: **high volume, high fiber,
  enough protein, low calorie density.** Swap the vegetables, the protein, or
  the spices and you'll still hit the same goal.
- Liquid-based meals like stews and soups feel fuller for longer at far fewer
  calories than a dry meal of the same size — your stomach senses volume, not
  just calories.
- Reheated the next day, the potatoes turn into resistant starch — great for
  satiety and a little better for you than fresh.
- Use low-sodium soy sauce so you control the final salt level yourself.

### コツ

- 正確なレシピより**構造**が大事 — 高ボリューム、高食物繊維、十分なタンパク質、
  低カロリー密度。野菜・タンパク質・スパイスを入れ替えても同じ目的を達成できる。
- シチューやスープのような液体ベースの食事は、同じ量の乾いた食事よりずっと少ない
  カロリーで長く満腹感が続く。胃はカロリーではなく「量」を感じ取る。
- 翌日に温め直すとじゃがいもがレジスタントスターチに変わり、満腹感が高まる。
- 減塩しょうゆを使い、最終的な塩分は自分で調整する。
