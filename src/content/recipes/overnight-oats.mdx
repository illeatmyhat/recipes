<div class="lang-en">

<ol class="steps">
<li><strong>Night before</strong><p>Combine oats and chia seeds in a container. Pour in kefir, add vanilla and salt. Stir well, cover, and refrigerate overnight (minimum 6 hours).</p></li>
<li><strong>Morning</strong><p>Scoop the oat mixture into a large bowl. It will be thick and creamy. Add a splash of kefir or water if you prefer a looser texture.</p></li>
<li><strong>Add fruit</strong><p>Top with fresh or thawed frozen fruit. Delicate fruits (banana, kiwi, raspberries) should always be added fresh — never the night before.</p></li>
<li><strong>Finish</strong><p>Add toppings for crunch, protein, or healthy fats. Add granola and seeds right before eating to preserve texture. Serve immediately.</p></li>
</ol>

### Tips

- Stays fresh for 3 days in the fridge. It gets tangier each day as the kefir
  cultures stay active.
- Frozen berries are nutritionally equivalent to fresh, and often cheaper.
- Too thick in the morning? Add a splash of water or kefir.
- Meal prep: store the oat-and-kefir base and the fruit separately, and add
  the fruit each morning.

### コツ

- 冷蔵庫で3日間保存可能。日を追うごとにケフィアの菌が活発で酸味が増す。
- 冷凍ベリーは栄養価がほぼ同等で、コストも安いことが多い。
- 朝に固すぎる場合は、水かケフィアを少量加える。
- 作り置きはオーツとケフィアのベースとフルーツを別々に保存し、毎朝フルーツを加える。
